Service to Scouting and Our Community

Service is at the heart of the Order of the Arrow. Nagatamen members give their time to support camps, Scouting units, the Yellow River District and our local communities.

Eagle Project Perpetual Care

Nagatamen helps preserve and care for Eagle Scout projects throughout the district. By returning to maintain, repair and improve these projects, the chapter helps the work of Eagle Scouts continue making an impact long after the original project is completed.
