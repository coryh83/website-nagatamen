Egwa Tawa Dee Lodge

Egwa Tawa Dee Lodge serves as the Order of the Arrow Lodge for the Atlanta Area Council, providing service and leadership opportunities throughout the region.

The Lodge plays a vital role in supporting Scouting events, maintaining camps, and organizing service projects.
