OUR LODGE

Egwa Tawa Dee

Nagatamen is the local OA chapter for the Yellow River District.

Chapter and Lodge

Nagatamen brings the Order of the Arrow close to home for Scouts in the Yellow River District. Egwa Tawa Dee Lodge brings Arrowmen together from across the council.

Lodge activities give members opportunities for larger service projects, fellowship, inductions, training and outdoor events. Nagatamen members are part of both the local chapter and the larger Lodge community.
