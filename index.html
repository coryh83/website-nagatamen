WHO ARE WE?

A chapter built around friendship, adventure and service.

Nagatamen is the Order of the Arrow chapter serving the Yellow River District. The OA recognizes Scouts who live the Scout Oath and Law and challenges its members to keep serving others.

For us, that means getting together, helping our Scouting community, spending time outdoors and giving youth a place to lead. It's not about sitting in meetings. It's about doing things together that matter.

GET OUT THERE

Some of the best memories happen outside.

Camping has always been a big part of Scouting and the Order of the Arrow. Nagatamen gives Arrowmen more reasons to get outside—from chapter campouts and Lodge weekends to service projects and bigger OA adventures.

Sometimes we're working. Sometimes we're learning. Sometimes we're just around a campfire with friends. Usually it's a little of all three.

LEAVE IT BETTER

CHEERFUL SERVICE

Making our corner of the world a little better.

Service is at the heart of the Order of the Arrow. Nagatamen members help our camps, our district and our local communities—not because we need recognition, but because somebody needs to do the work.

Eagle Project Perpetual Care

One of Nagatamen's continuing efforts is helping preserve and care for Eagle Scout projects throughout the district so the work of those Scouts keeps making an impact.

PART OF SOMETHING BIGGER

Nagatamen is your local chapter.
Egwa Tawa Dee is our Lodge.

Our chapter brings the OA close to home in the Yellow River District. The Lodge brings Arrowmen together from across the council for larger service projects, inductions, fellowship, training and outdoor events. Being part of Nagatamen connects you to both.
