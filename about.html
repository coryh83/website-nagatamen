Nagatamen Chapter

Who We Are

Nagatamen is the Order of the Arrow chapter serving the Yellow River District. The OA recognizes Scouts who live the Scout Oath and Law and challenges its members to continue serving others.

Our chapter gives local Arrowmen a place to build friendships, develop leadership, enjoy the outdoors and serve Scouting and our community.

What We Do

We get together for chapter activities, campouts, service projects, fellowship and Lodge events. Youth leadership is central to the chapter, with adults supporting them as advisers.
